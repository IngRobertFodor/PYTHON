3. TOK DAT (WORKFLOW) — DVOJFAZOVY SCORING
==============================================================

  --- FAZA 1: SCRAPING + LACNY PRE-SCORING (0 HTTP, okamzite) ---

  1. Pouzivatel nastavi kriteria (config/criteria.yaml alebo UI)
  2. scraper_service.scrape_all()
       -> 7 zdrojov paralelne (ThreadPoolExecutor):
            nehnutelnosti_sk, topreality_sk, reality_sk
            spf, ske_drazobne_vyhlasky, notarske_drazby
            obchodny_vestnik
       -> dedup podla (url + parcel_number)
       -> vysledok: list[Parcel] (typicky 2000-5000 kusov)
  3. scoring_service.preliminary_score() pre KAZDY pozemok (0 HTTP):
       price_score    (30%) -> cena v konfigurovanom rozsahu
       area_score     (25%) -> vymera v konfigurovanom rozsahu
       ppsm_score     (20%) -> EUR/m2 pod limitom
       dist_score     (10%) -> odhad vzdialenosti od BA z location_text
       source_bonus   (10%) -> drazbove zdroje = bonus (podhodnotene)
       [Normalizacia vah: chybajuci udaj = komponent vynechany, nie penalizacia]
     -> parcel.preliminary_score (0-100) + prelim_recommendation
        + prelim_confidence (0.0-1.0) + prelim_missing (zoznam)
        + prelim_breakdown (rozpis komponentov)
        + signals (investicne signaly, napr. BLIZKO_BA, DRAZBA_PODHODNOTENA)
     Verdikt: STRONG BUY / INVESTIGATE / CONSIDER / SKIP / NEDOSTATOK_DAT
     (NEDOSTATOK_DAT ak confidence < 0.5 -- malo dat na spolahlive skore)
  4. Zoradeny zoznam (tabulka) + filter (zdroj / cena max / vymera min)
     Pouzivatel zafajkne kandidatov (max 30)

  --- FAZA 2: PLNY GIS SCORING VYBRANYCH (HTTP, ~20s/parcelu) ---

  5. POST /api/scrape/score-selected {urls: [...]}
     Pre KAZDU vybranú parcelu -- pipeline_service.analyze_parcel():
       ensure_coordinates()     -> GPS suradnice (geocoding_service)
       run_gis_services()       -> 8 GIS sluzieb paralelne (feature flagy):
         distance_service       -> vzdialenost od BA (Haversine)
         flood_service          -> Q100 zaplavova zona (SHMU WMS)
         terrain_service        -> sklon, zosuvy, radon (DMR/SGUDSH)
         bpej_service           -> bonita pody, ochrana, odvody
         overpass_service       -> cesta, elektrina, voda, zastavba (building),
                                    classify_access (PRIAMA/CIASTOCNA/ZIADNA),
                                    classify_position (STRED/OKRAJ/MIMO),
                                    is_gem_candidate (okraj+priama dostupnost) okolie (OSM)
         protected_service      -> VVN/VTL, Natura 2000, CHKO
         zbgis_service          -> geometria (sirka, tvar, vymera)
       run_price_service()      -> price_analysis_service EUR/m2
  6. scoring_service.score_parcel() -> vazene skore 0-100 + recommendation
  7. Ak skore >= 70:
       cadastral_service        -> manualny LV checklist (B/C/D)
  8. report_service             -> investicny report + text
  9. notifier_service           -> email / Telegram (prah skore, feature flag)
  10. Mapa zobrazena len pre skórovane parcely (maju GPS)

  Odolnost pipeline:
    Vypnuta sluzba  -> ServiceResult.skip_result (neutralne 50)
    Vynimka sluzby  -> ServiceResult.error_result (pipeline pokracuje)

4. SCORING ALGORITMUS
==============================================================

  --- FAZA 1: LACNY PRE-SCORING (scoring_service.preliminary_score) ---

  Kriterium             | Vaha  | Zdroj dat
  ----------------------|-------|---------------------------
  Cena v rozsahu        | 30 %  | parcel.price_eur
  Vymera v rozsahu      | 25 %  | parcel.area_sqm
  EUR/m2 pod limitom    | 20 %  | parcel.price_per_sqm
  Vzdialenost od BA     | 10 %  | location_text (sk_places/GeoNames)
  Bonus drazbovy zdroj  | 10 %  | parcel.source_portal

  Normalizacia vah: ak vstup chyba, komponent sa VYNECHA (vaha 0).
  Chybajuci udaj nezniži skore -- reportuje sa ako "missing".
  Suspicious cena (<1.5 EUR/m2 z inzeratu) tiez vynecha ppsm komponent.

  Nove polia Parcel:
    prelim_confidence (0.0-1.0): podiel pouzitych vah (1.0 = vsetky data OK)
    prelim_missing (list):       co chybalo, napr. ["cena", "poloha"]
    prelim_breakdown (dict):     skore per komponent (None = vynechany)
    signals (list):              investicne signaly -- pozri sekciu 12

  Verdikt: STRONG BUY / INVESTIGATE / CONSIDER / SKIP
           alebo NEDOSTATOK_DAT ak confidence < 0.5

  -> parcel.preliminary_score (0-100) + prelim_recommendation
  Rozsahy z criteria.yaml: price.min/max_eur, parcel.min/max_area_sqm

  --- FAZA 2: PLNY GIS SCORING (scoring_service.score_parcel) ---

  Kriterium             | Vaha (predvolena, menitelna v YAML)
  ----------------------|--------
  Cena (EUR/m2)         | 18 %
  Vzdialenost od BA     | 12 %
  Infrastruktura        | 20 %
  Pravna proxy          | 18 %
  Zaplavove riziko      | 10 %
  Bonita pody (BPEJ)    |  8 %
  Teren                 |  7 %
  Chranene uzemia       |  7 %

  >= 85 -> STRONG BUY    >= 70 -> INVESTIGATE
  >= 50 -> CONSIDER      <  50 -> SKIP

  Vahy:   config/criteria.yaml -> scoring -> weights
  Prahy:  config/criteria.yaml -> scoring -> thresholds

12. INVESTICNA AWARENESS -- DETEKTOR SKVOSTOV
==============================================================

  Ciel: najst orna poda na okraji dediny s priamou dostupnostou infrastruktury
  -> potencialny stavebny pozemok (preklasifikacia v uzemnom plane = zisk)

  Implementovane v: backend/services/signals.py (gis_signals, preliminary_signals)
  Testy: tests/test_signals.py (30 testov, 100% na realnom tvare ServiceResult.data)
  Testy: tests/test_rezoning_service.py (16 testov)

  VRSTVA 1: Skvost kandidat (automaticky, po GIS scoringu)
    Vstupy zo sluzieb (presne kluce ServiceResult.data):
      zbgis_service:     data["is_agricultural"]            -> bool
      overpass_service:  data["village_position"]["position"] -> STRED/OKRAJ/MIMO
                         data["direct_access"]["level"]       -> PRIAMA/CIASTOCNA/ZIADNA
                         data["electricity"]["distance_m"]    -> float (m)
                         data["road"]["distance_m"]           -> float (m)
      bpej_service:      data["protection_class"]            -> int 1-9
                         data["estimated_odvody_eur_per_m2"] -> float

    Signaly (signals.py gis_signals):
      PREKLASIFIKACIA: is_agricultural + OKRAJ + {PRIAMA|CIASTOCNA} + bpej >= 5
      LACNE_VYNATIE:   is_agricultural + bpej >= 7 (odvody <= 0.10 EUR/m2)
      DRAHE_VYNATIE:   is_agricultural + bpej <= 4 (odvody >= 3 EUR/m2)

    Prelim signaly (signals.py preliminary_signals, 0 HTTP):
      BLIZKO_BA:                odhad km z sk_places < 25 km
      PRILIS_DALEKO:            odhad km z sk_places > 82 km
      CENA_PODOZRIVA:           inzerat + ppsm < 1.5 EUR/m2
      DRAZBA_PODHODNOTENA:      drazobny zdroj + cena v rozsahu
      PODIEL_ALEBO_CHYBNE_DATA: area_sqm < 100 ALEBO (price < 500 + area < 50)
                                  -> cap investment_index na max 30

    Historicke signaly (signals.py history_signals, z DB po save_run):
      DLHO_V_PONUKE:      inzerat > 120 dni v ponuke
      CENA_KLESA:         pokles ceny >= 5% od prvej hodnoty
      HORICE:             DLHO_V_PONUKE + CENA_KLESA + age >= 180 dni

    Drazobne signaly (signals.py drazba_signals, B2):
      DRAZBA_POD_ZNALECKOU: vyvolavacia >= 20% pod znaleckou
      DRAZBA_OPAKOVANA:     kolo >= 2 (predchadzajuce prebehlo bez zaujemcov)
